BRAND NEW FRAME // PRIVACY

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

Brand New Frame is a browser-based photo framer. The app is designed so that photos you select are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to a Brand New Frame server.

2. Photos and files

When you select an image, the browser creates a temporary local object URL so the image can be displayed, positioned, edited, previewed, and exported. The app does not send the image to a Brand New Frame backend or store it in a Brand New Frame database.

Temporary browser object URLs are released when they are no longer needed. Exported images are generated locally in the browser.

3. Information we collect

Brand New Frame does not intentionally collect account information, uploaded photos, EXIF metadata, analytics events, advertising identifiers, or payment information through the application itself.

The hosting provider and infrastructure used to serve the website may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata, and security logs as part of operating and protecting the service. Those practices are controlled by the applicable hosting provider.

4. Third-party services

The website currently loads the “Press Start 2P” font from Google Fonts. Loading that resource may cause your browser to make a request to Google. If this external resource is removed or self-hosted in a future version, this section will be updated accordingly.

5. Cookies and tracking

Brand New Frame does not intentionally set tracking cookies or use advertising trackers in the application.

6. Children's privacy

The application is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because photos are processed locally and there is no Brand New Frame account system, the app does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use the core framer.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's data practices or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date above will be changed when material updates are made.

8. Contact
